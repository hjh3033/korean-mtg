Magic in Korea, 1995–1998

A series by Jeff Hyun · new parts added as they are written

The collector's guide covers the facts: which sets, which borders, how to tell. This series is for everything that doesn't fit in a table — what it was actually like to play Magic in Busan in the late 1990s, where the Korean-language cards came from, and why so few of them made it out. Parts are short and will be added one at a time.
